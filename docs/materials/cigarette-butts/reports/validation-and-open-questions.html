Batch-01 validation requirements and open questions

Date: 2026-11-07. This is a desk-only evidence agenda accompanying the final batch-01 synthesis, not an experimental protocol or authorization. No experimental validation, no safety claim, no novelty claim, and no experiment authorized. No legal, patentability, freedom-to-operate, deployment or environmental-benefit determination is made.

Priority, evidence status, and independent gates

The primary revision target is J-H-01, a new unvalidated judge hypothesis; A-P-01/F3 is the strongest minority comparator when polymer quality controls the objective, while B-P-02 and B-P-03 remain secondary hypotheses rather than validated fallback processes (accepted Stage-4 judgment). B-P-01 and C-P-01/F1P retain their individual provenance but are represented in the primary agenda through J-H-01's explicitly new combination (proposal mapping and synthesis).

Source-supported means the read record states a mechanism or claim; inference means interpretation without demonstrated performance; new-assumption means an unmeasured performance, routing or usability proposition. Any unspecified numerical criterion, sample count, method performance, price, operating condition or partition value is parameter-not-located; no universal threshold is created.

The accepted evidence requirements separate overall dry-mass accounting, analyte fate, final treatment acceptance/performance and product-use performance rather than letting one favorable result substitute for the others (Stage-3 accepted distinctions). In particular, a closed balance can describe complete retention in product, and a permit or partner name cannot demonstrate treatment performance (Stage-4 gate requirements).

Shared minimum evidence envelope for any later authorized pilot

The following describes what evidence would be needed, not how to operate equipment. No pressure-vessel, solvent-recovery/distillation, caustic-pulping or hazardous-waste processing instructions are given.

  • Feed and basis: Define a bounded, traceable feed lot; report received wet mass, moisture/dry-matter basis, and whole-butt versus filter-only basis without silently switching denominators; map representative aliquots to total parent-stream mass or volume (judge's common feed requirement).
  • Analyte panel: Report nicotine, identified TSNAs including NNN/NNK where appropriate, representative PAHs, and each of Cd/Pb/As/Hg separately; route-relevant solvents and material-quality evidence are additional requirements, not substitutes (accepted common panel; Stage-4 route-specific requirements).
  • Stream totals and inventory: Measure analyte mass in product, every liquid and solid residual, captured emission/condensate, purge, treatment inlet and final outlet, as well as beginning/end recycle inventory; unexplained loss stays explicit (judge's all-stream requirement).
  • Analytical interpretability: Use matrix-appropriate methods with justified sampling, blanks, recovery, uncertainty and detection/quantitation reporting; do not treat below-quantitation results as zero or carry over worker sampling counts and illustrative method-performance values (judge's measurement corrections).
  • Product gate: Declare intended use and jurisdiction before judging contaminant content, leaching/migration, relevant emissions and material performance; any additional ecotoxicity requirement must be addressed separately, and no gate has yet been passed (independent product gate).
  • Treatment gate: Name the actual final route, document acceptance of the characterized stream and measure inlet-to-outlet analytical performance, including generated solids and air emissions; transfer to a treatment boundary alone is insufficient (four-field sink requirement).

Synthesis accounting convention, not an executed calculation: for analyte (i) in stream (s), let (m_{i,s}) be total analyte mass, derived from measured concentration and the complete stream mass or volume on the matching basis. The partition fraction is

[ f_{i,s}=\frac{m_{i,s}}{m_{i,\mathrm{feed}}}. ]

For a process boundary with opening and closing inventories (I_{i,0}) and (I_{i,1}), report the unresolved residual as

[ u_i=m_{i,\mathrm{external\ inputs}}+I_{i,0} -m_{i,\mathrm{external\ outputs}}-I_{i,1}. ]

These are inference-level bookkeeping definitions, not claimed results or acceptance thresholds. Internal recycle, treatment inlet and treatment outlet must not all be summed as independent final outputs; reconcile each sub-boundary separately, then reconcile the whole system. If parent analytes disappear, unexplained disappearance is not automatically detoxification or destruction; transformation evidence and relevant additional endpoints would be needed before making that inference.

Surviving hypotheses and decisive tests

J-H-01: primary, new unvalidated F1-based architecture

Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption: On a defined feed, analyte partition ranking can identify a controllable reject/purge boundary, with no indefinite recycle accumulation, accepted and analytically demonstrated final treatment, and a CA-rich output that independently meets a declared use gate (J-H-01 definition). Source-supported F1 separation/washing and an external-partner statement support candidate boundaries only; neither establishes this claim (frozen F1 reconstruction; Re-Cig company dossier).

  • Single most decisive measurement: Same-feed analyte-mass partition ranking across tobacco/ash/reject, candidate initial wash, later wash/purge and CA-rich product; rank total analyte mass per feed-analyte mass, not concentration alone (B-P-01 discriminator adopted by judge).
  • Minimal later pilot evidence: Apply the shared panel and dry-feed basis to feed, separated rejects, initial and later wash fractions, opening/closing recycle inventory, measured purge, product, fines, conditional treatment sludge, treatment inlet/outlet and captured drying emissions; independently assess the declared CA-product use and final residual route (J-H-01 measurement envelope).
  • Explicit falsifier: The selected wash-and-purge version is killed if it selects the wrong burden-bearing stream, produces liquid with no acceptable final route, fails demonstrated outlet requirements, or leaves product above its declared use criterion without a measured alternative separation; uncontrolled inventory growth or uncharacterized emissions also prevent support (judge's failure version).
  • Decision consequence: Reject-dominant partition can simplify or redirect J-H-01 rather than prove the whole conditional architecture impossible; retained metals failing the product gate require rejection of that product disposition, not an automatic lower-exposure sale or a claim that a use restriction solves contamination (judge's redirect and metals conditions).

A-P-01 / F3: preserved minority polymer-quality comparator

Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption: An additional separation/wash boundary after F3 polymer recovery can reduce retained contaminants while preserving use-relevant CA quality and keeping solvent, liquid, residue and emission streams accountable (judge's revised A-P-01). F3's family-level recovery logic is source-supported, but the proposed additional-stage selectivity and final treatment outcomes are not (frozen CN limits).

  • Single most decisive measurement: A linked before/after additional-boundary partition-and-polymer-quality balance: quantify analytes and recovered polymer across precipitate, wash/mother liquor and residue, and compare molecular-weight/quality evidence to the defined input and intended product requirement (A-P-01 falsifiers).
  • Minimal later pilot evidence: Apply the shared analyte panel and feed basis to pretreatment liquids, insolubles, precipitated CA, mother liquor, post-recovery wash, recovered solvent, purge, capture streams and final treatment residuals/outlets; separately report residual process solvents/acid, polymer molecular-weight distribution, identity/degree of substitution where relevant, leaching and intended-use material performance (F3 source gaps; judge's A-P-01 requirements).
  • Explicit falsifier: The added-boundary version fails if co-precipitation/metal retention or residual solvent causes a failed product-use gate, polymer quality cannot meet the declared function, or the recovery/residual system cannot be closed and routed; conductivity or appearance cannot reverse that failure (judge's A-P-01 disposition).
  • Dissent boundary: F3 is not settled by the F1 preference: it may be the stronger starting point under a polymer-quality objective, but Worker A's numerical operating assumptions, arbitrary partition thresholds and apparent measured sink entries are not adopted (minority preservation and corrections).

B-P-02: secondary metal-first F3 hypothesis

Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption: A distinct pre-product liquid phase can remove metals from the CA recovery path with useful selectivity, without unacceptable organic carryover, polymer-quality loss or an unmanageable residual system (B-P-02 as bounded by judge). Acid-phase metal transfer is an unmeasured premise, not a source-supported outcome of the CN recovery claim (Worker B premise).

  • Single most decisive measurement: A same-feed, phase-resolved balance for each of Cd/Pb/As/Hg across the candidate liquid, recovered CA and filtered insoluble residue; this directly tests the claimed metal destination (B-P-02 falsifier).
  • Minimal later pilot evidence: Use the shared panel and dry-feed basis across every pretreatment, liquid, residue, polymer, wash, recovered-solvent, purge, capture and treatment-outlet stream; add route-relevant speciation evidence, organic co-partition, reagent recovery, residual solvent/acid and the independent CA-use gate (judge's B-P-02 evidence needs).
  • Explicit falsifier: If metals do not leave the polymer path by the hypothesized liquid phase, the metal-first premise fails; a product failing its declared metal criterion or an unroutable metal-bearing residual also kills the proposed disposition (B-P-02 failure modes).
  • Inference limit: Failure of this one liquid-phase premise does not prove every possible CA-recovery process impossible; for example, metals transferred to a separately routable solid would require different accounting and a different hypothesis, not automatic rejection of all polymer recovery.

B-P-03: secondary F5 liquor-purge and precipitate hypothesis

Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption: F5 conversion can be paired with a measured liquor purge and independently characterized precipitate so that contaminants do not accumulate, final liquid/solid routes perform as required, and cellulose product independently meets its intended-use gate (judge's B-P-03 definition). The source-supported family description does not establish pulp purity, liquor treatability or precipitate disposal (frozen F5 reading limits).

  • Single most decisive measurement: A cycle-resolved analyte inventory balance across pulp, liquor, purge and precipitate, including treatment outputs; this tests whether the proposed contaminant exit actually prevents accumulation (B-P-03 loop falsifier; judge's bounded requirement).
  • Minimal later pilot evidence: Use the shared panel and dry whole-butt/filter basis for feed, converted dry pulp, dark liquor, opening/closing loop inventory, wash water, purge, precipitate, captured emissions and final treatment inlet/outlet/residuals; add COD/TOC and relevant salt/phenolic loading without substituting those bulk measures for individual toxicants, and separately assess cellulose identity, function and use-specific leaching/contact evidence (F5 unresolved streams; B-P-03 evidence requirements).
  • Explicit falsifier: Continued target-analyte accumulation despite the specified purge/treatment boundary, failed pulp-use criteria or no acceptable final liquor/precipitate route kills the stated version; a large necessary purge would refute an efficient-recycle assumption without alone disproving deacetylation chemistry (B-P-03 failure distinctions; judge's disposition).
  • Document prerequisite: The Teixeira paper/thesis and primary patent remain necessary to distinguish measured conditions and treatment outcomes from abstract-level retrieval leads; no literature removal number is imported as a design constant (accepted retrieval-lead limitation).

Jurisdiction and regulatory acceptance gaps

  • Selected jurisdiction and material status: The project has not resolved the governing feed/output waste classification, transport, storage, treatment permissions or End-of-Waste basis for a selected plant and product; French feed-classification leads cannot simply be exported to every country or output (gap G-19; judge's classification distinction).
  • Actual final recipient: For each liquid, solid and capture residual, the permitted recipient, actual acceptance specification, treatment residuals, discharge basis and demonstrated analytical outlet remain unresolved; “external purification” alone answers none of these fully (Stage-4 sink standard).
  • Intended-use criteria: Exact contact, leaching/migration, emissions, ecotoxicity and performance acceptance bases are unresolved for the proposed CA/cellulose articles, compost and composites; no universally applicable product limit or automatic downgrade-to-restricted-use rule is adopted (judge's independent product gate).
  • Standards and hazard-property extrapolation: Worker B's HP6/HP14 and compost-standard discussion includes search-excerpt and secondary material; neither numerical standard limits nor an assertion that nicotine removal alone clears a legal hazard property is accepted here without authoritative, material-specific verification (Worker B access disclosure; judge's bounded use of that material).
  • Equipment, exposure and intellectual-property review: Solvent, chemical, pressure, air-emission and occupational obligations remain qualified-engineering/legal questions; patent-status shorthand, term arithmetic and public disclosure are not novelty, patentability or freedom-to-operate determinations (charter-aligned limits in the accepted judge report).

Preserved dissent, metadata concern, and stop conditions

F3 remains an explicit minority comparator, not a settled loser; a change in the controlling objective from sink testability to polymer quality can change which hypothesis merits attention (Stage-4 dissent). The wrong-stream/dilute-purge objection, unsolved metals, F2 dilution/off-gas warning and absence of comparable scale evidence all survive into this agenda (preserved objections).

The hash-normalization concern is also not silently closed: the repaired manifest calls the hashes raw committed-byte hashes, but the local Stage-5 git show audit matches 6/8 Stage-4 entries as raw blobs and matches the remaining two only after LF-to-CRLF conversion preserving their BOM (repaired manifest assertion being tested; Worker B's original concern). Audit denominator: matching file entries / hashed Stage-4 payload entries; the exceptions are preserved Stage-3 worker-a.md and worker-b_Tencent-Hy4.md, and this is a provenance check rather than process evidence.

The companion synthesis records exact raw-blob digests and the session log records the reproduction method. Packet/panel bytes and acceptance statements remain unchanged; metadata reconciliation is an operator-owned follow-up, not permission to reopen scientific inputs, claim payload drift or alter a chemistry verdict.

Any proposal may fail, and no successful invention count is required (judge's final dissent boundary). Missing quantitation, a failed product gate, no acceptable residual route, treatment refusal/failure or uncharacterized loss prevents the corresponding claim from being supported; uncertainty is not averaged away by a favorable result elsewhere.

Boundary conditions and operator handoff

No experiment, pilot, company contact, purchase or physical validation is authorized by this report. All process-performance claims remain hypothesis-level pending measurement, and no safety/legal/novelty/deployment or environmental-benefit determination is made.

The immediate next desk priority, if separately commissioned, is full-text verification of FR3066128B1 to separate F1 disclosure from J-H-01 assumptions, while preserving F3 dissent and the unresolved metadata record (judge's nominated next desk step). That retrieval is not a further stage automatically launched by this report; Stage 5 ends with scoped desk synthesis and operator review.
