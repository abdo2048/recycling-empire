Batch-01 cigarette-butt recycling: final desk synthesis

Batch-01 cigarette-butt recycling: final desk synthesis

Date: 2026-11-07. Scope: batch-01 only, process families F0–F5, accepted Stage-3 evidence review and accepted Stage-4 hypothesis generation. Desk synthesis only: no experimental validation, no safety claim, no novelty claim, and no experiment authorized. This report makes no patentability, freedom-to-operate, legal, deployment, or environmental-benefit determination.

Synthesis position and decision boundary

The best-supported conclusion is an evidence boundary, not a successful recycling process: no family is proven clean, safe, deployable, or environmentally beneficial, and none jointly establishes dry-mass closure, analyte-resolved fate, final treatment performance, and an independently passed use-specific product gate (accepted Stage-3 synthesis). The strongest individual Stage-4 proposal is B-P-01; the primary revision target is J-H-01, a new unvalidated judge hypothesis, combining its F1-based partition-ranking architecture with measured purge, separate reject/sludge boundaries, treatment acceptance and outlet evidence, and an independent product gate (Stage-4 judgment).

F3 remains the strongest minority alternative when recoverable cellulose-acetate quality is the controlling objective; this is dissent, not a settled winner or a validated comparator (Stage-4 dissent). Metals remain the hardest analyte class across the proposals because none demonstrates a metals-removal outcome, a use-compliant product, or a complete lawful metals sink (Stage-4 metals assessment).

Research question, scope, and evidence semantics

The investigation asks which cigarette-butt recycling claims survive the available public evidence and, from that evidence, which modified route could plausibly produce a usable output together with a characterized, lawful toxic-fraction sink, and what would falsify it (Stage-3 question as resolved; Stage-4 question as resolved). This is a purpose-bounded synthesis of the selected batch, not a worldwide inventory, exhaustive prior-art search, or ranking of all recycling technologies.

The evidentiary baseline is Stage-3 packet v1.1 at payload commit 5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774 and Stage-4 packet v1.0 at payload commit 671de7c045385dade4b8f26c7b1680a2cc3a809c; release metadata and later panel outputs are distinct from those frozen payloads (accepted workflow record; Stage-4 manifest). Both operator acceptance statements were read verbatim: acceptance closes the evidence-review and hypothesis-generation handoffs with REVISE carried forward, not the scientific validation of any proposal (operator acceptances).

Claims below use the following distinctions. Proposed measurements are evidence requirements, not test results or authorization.

  • Source-supported: A read reconstruction, panel, or source states the claim; this does not establish reproduction or independent verification.
  • Inference: A mechanistic or integration interpretation drawn from the record, not a measured outcome.
  • New-assumption: A performance, partition, routing, or product-use proposition introduced by a worker or judge; it remains falsifiable and unvalidated.
  • Unresolved: Evidence is missing, access is partial, denominators conflict, or a source has not been read; missing values are parameter-not-located, not zero.

Primary texts not obtained in the earlier stages were not newly retrieved for this synthesis. Citations identify the actual read project evidence and its reading limits, not a claim that this session independently read the underlying patents, regulatory texts, or laboratory reports. The judge's working files were read for provenance only, not as additional scientific evidence.

Accepted batch-level findings

  • Dry-mass balance: No family has a complete dry-basis process balance with compatible whole-butt/filter and input/output denominators; company accounting and composition splits do not supply one (Stage-3 accepted findings).
  • Analyte-resolved closure: No family closes nicotine, identified TSNAs, representative PAHs and Cd/Pb/As/Hg across product, residual liquids/solids, emissions, purge, treatment outlet and unexplained loss (common discriminator).
  • Sink overstatement: A named wash liquor, concentrate, external partner or recycle loop is not a demonstrated final sink; stream naming, collection boundary, final treatment/disposal and analytical performance must be recorded separately (four-field resolution).
  • Conflated endpoints: Identity, recovery, purity, detoxification, leaching and use performance are distinct; limited identity/material characterization does not amount to jointly passing those gates (Stage-3 endpoint distinctions).
  • Scale and environmental comparison: Comparable energy, reagent, cost, throughput and audit-ready environmental inventories are absent, so no deployment or environmental ranking follows (Stage-3 scale objection).
  • Secondary cautionary evidence: The Korean paving-stone and Keenat LCA-retreat accounts trigger product and environmental questions; they do not prove every composite or entire family fails, and their primary study/report remains a retrieval gap (accepted cautionary-evidence resolution).

What hypothesis generation produced

A candidate architecture with unacceptable product burden, no acceptable final treatment route, failed outlet performance or uncharacterized emissions is rejected or redirected; no successful variant is required, and any added chemistry is another new hypothesis (J-H-01 falsifiers).

Coherent primary architecture without a process claim

J-H-01 is best represented as a conditional evidence flow, not an operating flow sheet: characterize feed and separated fractions; rank analyte mass by stream; identify the controlling reject/liquid/product burden; assess a candidate measured-purge and residual-treatment boundary; and separately assess the intended product use (J-H-01 decision architecture). If the controlling burden is in tobacco/ash, a fixed initial-wash bleed should not be defended; if it remains in product, a named purge cannot rescue a failed product disposition (wrong-stream objection).

No equal-performance DIY substitute is established because neither performance criteria nor validated containment/analytical equivalence is available; no equipment build, hazardous handling procedure or operating recipe is supplied.

Dissent and unresolved provenance

  • F3 minority: The primary F1 choice optimizes testability of sink architecture, not polymer quality; F3 may be preferable under the latter objective, subject to co-precipitation, metals, solvent closure and independent product evidence (preserved minority position).
  • Wrong-stream and dilute-effluent objection: An initial-wash purge may miss the controlling tobacco/ash or product burden and create a larger unroutable liquid; only analyte-specific mass ranking, not concentration alone, discriminates those versions (B-P-01 adversarial screen).
  • Metals objection: No washing, phase-transfer or conversion proposal demonstrates metals compliance; thermal treatment is not elemental destruction, and species-specific fate cannot be replaced with a blanket volatility claim (judge's metals stop condition; frozen F2 reasoning limits).
  • F2 dilution/off-gas warning: A lower product concentration can arise from matrix dilution while less-volatile contaminants remain and volatile material exits in unmeasured air; this warning is not removed by selecting J-H-01 (accepted Stage-3 warning).
  • Scale objection: F1 cannot be called cheapest, F5 most expensive or any family deployment-ready from this record; incompatible inventories, functions and throughput bases block those rankings (Stage-4 scale restraint).
  • Classification versus product evidence: Feed hazard characterization is not an automatic legal classification of every output, and a chemical panel does not necessarily resolve all ecotoxicity or exposure endpoints; actual jurisdiction and intended use remain open (Stage-4 classification distinction).

What is not established and final handoff

No experiment or pilot was performed in this synthesis, and the accepted record supplies no comparable cost/energy dataset, verified permit for a proposed route, passed product-use gate or demonstrated detoxification (Stage-4 evidence limits). No claim is made that no company has such evidence privately; the conclusion is limited to what was located, read and accepted in this batch.

The companion report, reports/final/validation-and-open-questions.md, defines falsifiable claims, decisive evidence needs and unresolved acceptance bases without authorizing their execution. Stage 5 closes scoped desk synthesis for operator review, not a new research gate or permission to experiment; no validation, no safety claim, no novelty claim, and no experiment authorized remain the controlling boundaries.
