0. Summary card

TchaoMegot (Julien Paque, France) cleans cellulose acetate fibers recovered from butts with supercritical CO2 in a closed loop: CO2 is pressurized and heated past its critical point (>=73.85 bar, >=31 degC), contacted with the fibers for 1 min-8 h (jets, soaking, drums, optional ultrasound), then depressurized — the CO2 re-liquefies and recirculates while extracted contaminants collect as a small concentrate. Optional water/alcohol co-solvents widen polarity coverage; optional calixarene extractants target inorganic species. Outputs are clean fibers for thermal/acoustic insulation boards (with ~15% Trevira bicomponent binder) and filter/plastic applications. Priority because it is the only contaminant-removal route in the registry that leaves the polymer intact and uses no liquid waste stream, and the family is GRANTED in part (FR3102075B1 use patent granted 14.11.2025; FR3102076B3 utility certificate granted 22.10.2021). It is also the only route where the primary documents contain ZERO quantitative performance data — every removal number is a gap. The "99.7%/0.3%" split and "50/60 bar" press quotes in circulation are NOT in the patents. 123

1. What the sources disclose — step by step

1 = FR3102180A1 (filed 28.01.2020, priority 22.10.2019 FR1911822; applicant/inventor PAQUE Julien; A1 published 23.04.2021; search report X-cites EP0595184A1 against claims 1,2,4-7,23). 2 = FR3102075B1, granted divisional (14.11.2025), holder TCHAOMEGOT COLLECTE SAS — USE claims (insulation/filters/biodegradable plastics). 3 = FR3102076B3, the priority filing granted as Certificat d'Utilite (22.10.2021) — widest windows + only in-family residue estimate. 4 = FR3125946B1 (2026) — connected urban ashtray: supply-chain logistics, no process numbers.

  1. Pre-processing — triage, grinding, sieving (repeatable), optional friction/sieving/cycloning; only acetate fiber proceeds; other waste (leaves, tobacco, ashes) "recycled as compost". mesh sizes, grinding parameters (evidence: parameter not located) 1
  2. Loading — fibers into a closed chamber/autoclave; capacity 0.2 L to 1500 L ("not limited"). 1
  3. Pressurization — 0-2000 bar, preferred 50-350 bar, most preferred 280-320 bar (claim 5); for CO2 specifically >=73.85 bar (critical pressure; ¶0040). 1
  4. Heating — 0-400 degC, preferred 15-80 degC (claim 5); for CO2 >=31 degC (critical temperature). 1
  5. Optional humidification — water as co-solvent "solubilizes nicotine more easily"; no moisture % given. 1
  6. Contact (S2_4) — high-pressure spray, soaking, jet nozzles; jet speed 1-500 m/s, flow 1-4000 L/h; duration 1 min - 8 h; optional agitation aids (rotary drum, vibration/ultrasound, compression, mixer, centrifuge) — no frequencies/powers/durations given. 1
  7. Optional co-solvent (S2_5) — menu: water, ethanol, methanol, butanol, terpenes, benzene, cyclohexanes, ketones, hydrofluoroethers; added between pressurization and heating; "increases polarity and selectivity ... sugars, ions"; no doses. High-pressure development ">350 bars" (S3: ">400 bars") allows extraction of more polar molecules WITHOUT co-solvents. 1
  8. Optional extractant (S2_6) — "calixarenes" or complexing/chelating agents for inorganic species; no identity detail, no doses. 1
  9. Depressurization and recovery — pressure let-down -> separator releases gaseous CO2 -> condenser re-liquefies -> reservoir; continuous loop; optional activated-carbon filter for odors/VOCs; extracted contaminants valorized elsewhere. 1
  10. Outputs — cleaned fiber; insulation boards via bicomponent binder "Trevira 255 2.2 dtex/6 mm (PES co-polyethylene)", 15% binder / 85% acetate fiber; plastics processing "80-300 degC"; no drying step (fiber exits dry). 1
  11. S3-only numbers — board curing 170 degC / 15 min (binder shell melts at 127 degC); toxic-substance concentrate "estimated at a few grams per 10 kg treated" (approx. 0.03-0.1 wt% — the nearest in-family basis for the circulated "0.3%"); one coat's insulation ~4,000 recycled butts; biodegradability criterion: 90% dry mass in <6 months. 3
  12. Contaminant target list — nicotine, heavy metals, organic acids, nitrosamines, fungicides (flumetraline, pendimethalin, trifluraline), phenols, PAHs, formaldehyde/acetaldehyde, dioxins/furans, anions; waste class HP14 ecotoxic + HP6 acute toxicity. No quantitative removal data in ANY of the four documents — all performance statements are qualitative ("a very large majority, even the totality"). 1

2. Equipment

  • SC-CO2 extraction plant: pressurizable chamber 0.2-1500 L, CO2 pump, heater, jet/spray manifold, optional drum/ultrasound agitation, let-down valve, separator, condenser, CO2 reservoir, activated-carbon guard filter 1
  • Pre-processing: grinder, sieve, cyclone 1
  • Board line: fiber blending with Trevira 255 bicomponent fiber, oven cure 170 degC / 15 min 1
  • Scale note: even a 5-20 L lab SC-CO2 extractor is specialized equipment with certified pressure vessels — student-lab access is unlikely; partner-facility work is the realistic path. The charter's no-DIY-pressure-vessel rule binds absolutely here.

1 Operating point inside 280-320 bar / 15-80 degC ai proposal: 300 bar / 45 degC, 30-60 min contact, 2-3 cycles per batch Mid-range of the preferred window maximizes margin on both axes; CO2 density ~0.8-0.9 g/cm3 there — good solvation; nicotine is moderately polar, needs the high-pressure band or cosolvent. Partner-facility run: residual nicotine (HPLC) in fiber vs time/cycles; plateau = sufficient.
2 Co-solvent choice and dose ai proposal: ethanol 5 vol% of CO2 Patent lists ethanol without dose; 1-10 vol% is the conventional SC-CO2 modifier band; ethanol is the least hazardous menu item that raises polarity for nicotine/phenols. Compare plain CO2 vs +5% ethanol on nicotine + phenol removal; if no gain at 5%, test 10% or drop cosolvent.
3 Chelating extractant (calixarenes) ai proposal: skip in first trials — run plain CO2 and measure metals first Calixarenes are expensive specialty molecules; SC-CO2 alone does not remove ionic/metals species without modifiers — the patent itself admits inorganic extraction needs extractants. ICP-MS of fiber before/after plain-CO2 run. Expect little change -> metals handled by the feedstock-cleaning upstream step, not SCCO2.
4 Humidification level ai proposal: pre-condition fibers to 10-15% moisture Water is named as nicotine co-solvent without a dose; 10-15% is a typical fiber conditioning level that aids polar extraction without free water pooling. Pair runs dry vs conditioned; nicotine removal delta.
5 Residue concentrate quantity Conflict kept: S3 "a few grams per 10 kg" (~0.03-0.1 wt%) vs circulated press figure 0.3% — neither is a measurement; parameter not located pending team-scale weigh The B3 figure is an in-document estimate; the press figure is not in any patent. 4. Safety notes

  • High-pressure equipment only: certified autoclave, pressure relief devices, trained operators, never improvised — the charter's no-DIY-pressure-vessel rule is absolute for this route. 300 bar stored energy is lethal-class hazard. 1
  • CO2 asphyxiation: let-down and venting in ventilated area; O2 monitors for enclosed spaces.
  • Ethanol co-solvent under pressure: flammable; grounded equipment, explosion-rated electricals where required.
  • Concentrate handling: the extracted paste concentrates nicotine, PAHs, pesticides (HP6/HP14 per 1) — small mass, high hazard; sealed containers, licensed disposal.
  • Feedstock upstream: fiber cleaning assumes prior removal of tobacco/ash — that stream is itself HP-classified and needs lawful handling. 1
  • Stop-condition: metals uncharacterised in cleaned fiber (row 6).
  • Desk research authorizes no physical trial; qualified facility, risk assessment, lawful waste handling, named human actor.

5. What the team must verify in lab

Realistically via a partner facility with a certified SC-CO2 extractor (5-20 L):

  1. Decontamination truth test (rows 1, 2, 4, 8): 3-point matrix — plain CO2 300 bar/45 degC/30 min vs +5% ethanol vs humidified; residual nicotine + phenols + PAHs in fiber; weigh + assay the separator concentrate (row 5). THE experiment: fills the family's total quantitative void.
  2. Metals gate (rows 3, 6): ICP-MS on cleaned fiber; decides whether chelation is ever needed.
  3. Board trial (row 9): 15% Trevira blend, 170 degC/15 min cure; thermal conductivity + fire screening.
