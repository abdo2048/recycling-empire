C-01 cnr-mask-filters

CNR (Italy) patented crushing butts and recovering cellulosic material for respiratory-mask filters (IT202000003745A1, WO2021170381A1, EP3892393A1). Registry holds only the abstract-level "crush, separate, recover" disclosure — no process numbers extracted. parameter not located: disinfection chemistry, fiber specs, filtration performance, whether mask-grade material ever passed any standard. Family is pandemic-era (2020 priority). Status: pending_deep_dive. Verify: full EP3892393A1 spec read; any commercialization trace.

C-02 cerdia-ca-recovery

Cerdia International (DE, filter-maker) filed WO2026074041A2 (2024 priority) for recovering cellulose acetate from post-consumer filters including heat-not-burn mouthpieces. ai inference: industrially the most credible CA-recovery claimant in the registry because the applicant already operates acetate tow plants. parameter not located: full spec unextracted (newest filing on the shelf). Status: pending_deep_dive. Verify: full WO spec read — the single highest-value unextracted shelf item.

C-03 huang-us10980272

Miniwiz (Taiwan) apparatus patent US10980272B2 (granted, expires ~2039-06-04): isolating cellulose acetate from cigarette waste as machine train. Status: primary-source-read (evidence in archive/dossier). parameter not located: throughput, purity, decontamination performance — apparatus patents often disclose structure without chemistry.

C-04 burich-us7560059

Blake Burich process US7560059B2 / WO2007035749A2 (granted 2009) — early butt recycling process. No mechanism class extracted; parameter not located: everything. Status: pending_deep_dive. Expired-or-near check needed for freedom-to-operate.

C-05 us20140287144-abandoned

US20140287144A1: CA manufacturing waste dispersed in acetone, formed, pelletized. ABANDONED — free to use. Note: it covers FACTORY acetate waste, not post-consumer butts; the decontamination burden differs completely. Status: pending_deep_dive (short spec read would close it).

C-06 us5504119-expired

US5504119A (1994): recovery of cellulose esters from cigarette FACTORY waste streams. EXPIRED — free to use; useful as prior-art backbone for any team solvent route. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-07 cn106928486

Zhejiang/Ningbo CN106928486A/B (granted 2020): acetic-acid-based CA recovery. Registry claims 58-92% recovery — patent assertion only, denominator unresolved (batch-01 synthesis flagged: "not a detoxification or purity percentage"). Machine-translation extraction still owed. Status: primary-source-read.

C-08 cn111822484

Yunnan Changyi CN111822484A: acetate filter-rod regeneration. Chinese-language, unextracted; parameter not located: process, reagents, status. Lowest-effort card: one spec read closes it.

C-09 cn111118670-carbon-fiber

Inner Mongolia Kunming Cigarette Co, CN111118670A: carbon fiber from butts. Overlaps the peer-reviewed carbon line in M-07 (pyrolysis/carbonization family); ai inference: industrial applicability of "carbon fiber from mixed butt waste" is doubtful given feed heterogeneity. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-10 cn107946082-graphene

CN107946082A: cigarette-filter/graphene composite. Unextracted. Treat as materials-family (M-07 companion line adjacency). Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-11 cn108807014-supercapacitor

CN108807014: supercapacitor electrodes from butts. Mirrors the peer-reviewed 330.1 F/g N-doped carbon result already verified in M-07's companion line. ai inference: patent adds little beyond the published papers. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-12 kr20160005298

KR20160005298A: extraction of useful compounds from butts. KR-only, unextracted. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-13 kalabak-ethanol-fuel

Fevzi Kalabak (TR) WO2020231362A1: wash butts in water then ethanol; tar dissolves; tar-ethanol mixture as fuel additive. Quantities 5-320 g/L, mixing 5-60 min — the recipe is fully published (EP pending). ai inference: chemically plausible as an extraction but the fuel-additive endpoint is unvalidated (same concern class as M-07 row 2: an uncharacterized extract is not a fuel product). Status: pending_deep_dive — cheap to deep-dive (recipe already public).

C-14 matteri-ep2520184a1

Matteri Fabio TT Srl (IT) EP2520184A1 (2011 filing; deemed withdrawn 2013-05-08 — free to use): residual-tobacco removal + washing of butts. parameter not located: washing solution composition, temperature, duration. Status: primary-source-read (archive/dossier evidence exists).

C-15 kr-microbial-composting

KR10-1782684-1 (granted 2018): microbial composting device with odor removal. ai inference: biodegradation of CA is slow/unproven (CA's acetyl groups resist microbial attack; litter studies show decades-long persistence) — the patent's decomposition claim needs strains, time, temperature data, none extracted. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-16 academic-green-ethanol-wash

Peer-reviewed staged water/ethanol CA cleaning: hot water 50 degC 60 min -> 3x cold rinse -> 2x 99% ethanol; output clean CA (PMC7659942). No known patent block. This is the most lab-replicable open cleaning recipe in the registry — candidate to fold into any team washing train (M-01/M-02 adjacency). Status: pending_deep_dive but recipe-level already public.

C-17 academic-acetic-acid-route

2025 peer-reviewed acetic-acid selective solubilization of CA (Waste Management S0956053X25003265). Green-solvent family; overlaps C-07 chemistry with academic rigor. Status: pending_deep_dive — abstract-level only.

C-18 academic-ca-membranes

CA membranes from butts via dissolution (Sci Rep s41598-022-24432-x; Sci Total Environ S0048969724028249). High-value end-use claim; parameter not located: membrane performance specs vs virgin-CA membranes. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-19 cigarette-butt-asphalt

RMIT line: bitumen-encapsulated CBs in stone-mastic asphalt; 10-15 kg CB/m3; encapsulation (105 degC/24 h dry, 150 degC bitumen pot) required because CBs are toxic; strength decreases with CB content. Registry cites ceramics.org + Mohajerani 2017. Shares the M-08 leaching logic (encapsulation = the same bitumen pre-test seen in Kurmus Ch.5). Status: pending_deep_dive; verify: Marshall stability numbers, long-term road performance.

C-20 concrete-additive

Butts as concrete additive (unach.mx source). Registry-level only; ai inference: organic inclusion in concrete is water-absorption/degradation-risk territory; without a specific study read, treat as weakest construction card. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-21 3d-printing-filament

Filaret (EE startup) + academic: recovered CA to 3D-printing filament (3dprinting.com coverage). parameter not located: filament specs, printing parameters, decontamination train. ai inference: CA needs plasticizer/drying control for FDM — plausible but unproven. Status: pending_deep_dive.

C-22 cnr-knowledge-share-ca-recovery

CNR + AzzeroCO2 patent OFFERING (knowledge-share.eu): acetone dissolution of CA + nicotine extraction; filtration of paper/ash/tobacco; water anti-solvent precipitation of CA; acetone recovery by boiling; pollutant-water treatment; claimed reuse of the solid for spectacle frames, masks, shields, strings. Status: experimentally-supported-in-source (archive/dossier evidence exists), but offering-stage: TRL unverified. parameter not located: acetone-to-butt ratio, anti-solvent ratio, yield, purity, economics. Overlaps M-02 (De Fenzo acetone line) and C-01 (CNR mask use) — treat as the CNR ecosystem's technology-transfer face. Adjacent shelf data point (Shah 2023, Huang 2015, not in registry): recovered-CA fibrous membrane for Li-ion separators — tensile 34.1 MPa, porosity 66%, thermal stability to 200 degC — the highest-spec published use of recovered CA fibers, cross-checks C-11/C-18.
