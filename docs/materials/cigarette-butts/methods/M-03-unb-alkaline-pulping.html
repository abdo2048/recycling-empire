0. Summary card

Universidade de Brasilia's patented, peer-reviewed route: whole collected butts (filter + paper + tobacco + ash, no pre-sorting) are cooked in dilute NaOH at boiling temperature for 3 h. The alkali hydrolyzes cellulose acetate into cellulose (deacetylation), dissolves ash and smoke residues, liberates tobacco cellulose from lignin, and disaggregates paper; after filtering, the solid is cellulose pulp usable pure or blended in recycled paper, and the liquid is a "dark liquor" with extreme nicotine content that the authors manage by acid-precipitating lignin and reusing the clarified effluent in later cooks. The process was licensed to Poiato Recicla (Sao Paulo), whose pilot plant started March 2016 and sells pulp to a cardboard factory (company operation per the paper). Priority because it is the only peer-reviewed route that handles UNSORTED whole butts at pilot scale with published paper-quality results, its chemicals are commodity NaOH/HCl, and the Brazilian patent has expired by either term interpretation (computed) — free to use. 12

1. What the sources disclose — step by step

1 = BR 0305004 B1 granted spec; 2 = Teixeira et al. 2017 Waste Management 60:140-150. The patent defines the legal envelope (claim ranges); the paper is the operating protocol with numbers.

  1. Feedstock — unsorted butts from campus collectors (mixed brands, variable tobacco/paper, lipstick and saliva residues included); filter-production scraps used as a clean variant. 2
  2. Cooking / pulping — pilot: 50 L stainless steel reactor, heated by LPG flame, digital thermocouple; NaOH 1% in water "to catalyze the reaction"; 3 h at boiling temperature. 2 Patent examples: 233 g used filters in 15 L of NaOH 1% at 75-85 degC for 3 h; 467 g CA scraps in 15 L at 75-85 degC, 3 h. 1 Claim envelope: 10 min-24 h; 20-140 degC; catalyst 0-50% by mass (acids OR bases: mineral/organic acids, amines, ammonia, hydroxides/carbonates/oxides of NH4, Na, K, Ca, Mg, Li); pressure 0-100 atm. Preferred per spec: 80-100 degC, most preferably ~80 degC; catalyst 1-5% relative to water mass. 1 Conflicting temperatures (boiling vs 75-85 vs 80) are all kept — see row 3.
  3. Alkaline hydrolysis kinetics (FT-IR, 80 degC) — distilled water 6 h: acetate C=O bands (1760/1730 cm-1) unchanged; NaOH 0.5%: bands partially decrease at 10 min, gone by 20 min (150 min identical); NaOH 1%: gone in 10 min. Conclusion of source: deacetylation is fast and rate depends on NaOH concentration. 2
  4. Filtration — mixture filtered after cooking; solid = cellulose pulp; liquid = dark liquor. 2 filter medium, pore size, washing steps, number of washes (evidence: parameter not located)
  5. Refining and sheet making (product QC in source) — Jokro mill 25 min; Schopper-Riegler 46 degSR (pulped butts) vs 14 degSR per paper Table 4 for untreated CA filter — the paper's running text says 15 degSR and the patent's Example 2 says 15 degSR; 14 vs 15 is a source inconsistency, both kept. TAPPI sheet former; pulped-butt sheet: grammage 71.14 (1.98) g/m2, thickness 0.173 (0.004) mm, whiteness 54.16 (0.20)%, tensile strength 0.80 (0.08) kN/m, traction index 11.22 (1.83) kNm/kg, stretch 1.91 (0.23)%; untreated-CA sheet: 67.2 g/m2, 0.147 mm, 84.8% whiteness, 0.07 kN/m, 1.09 kNm/kg, 0.67% stretch — i.e., untreated CA makes mechanically worthless paper; alkaline cooking is what makes it paper. CA-scrap pulp sheet: 77.3 g/m2, 0.239 mm, 84.61% whiteness, 0.40 kN/m, 4.95 kNm/kg, 1.42%. 2
  6. Dark liquor characterization (35 cooks) — COD 29,986 (7,037) mg/L; BOD 9,548 (2,739) mg/L; BOD/COD 0.3 (chemical, not biological, treatment indicated); pH 8.5 (0.4); temperature 33.1 (3.1) degC; settleable solids 0.8 mL/L. Nicotine 35,400,000 ng/mL = 35.4 g/L; NNK 3,057 ng/mL; NNN 1,635 ng/mL. Metals by ICP-OES: As 0.04 mg/L, Cr 0.01 mg/L; Cd, Ni, Pb, Se not detected — all below CONAMA 430/2011 discharge limits. No carbonyls detected (volatility). 2
  7. Dark liquor treatment — acidification pH 1-9 with HCl 6 N (H2SO4 avoided: Brazilian sales controls); best COD removal at pH 3: 20.1%; FT-IR confirms lignin precipitation; clarified effluent COD ~23,959 mg/L. Coagulation of clarified effluent (jar test 180 rpm 2 min -> 60 rpm 15 min -> settle 20 min): Al2(SO4)3 0.10-0.30 g/L or chitosan 0.05-0.80 g/L; best chitosan 0.80 g/L at pH 9 -> 66.6% COD removal, residual 10,015 mg/L. Ozonation (4 h, 0.5 L/min, 1 mg O3/min, pH 9-12): ineffective, COD often rose. Nicotine: not detected after coagulation; NNN not detected after 2 h ozone; NNK 0.8 ng/mL after 4 h ozone. 2
  8. Effluent reuse loop (the source's answer to disposal) — clarified effluent (pH 3) is re-neutralized to pH 7 and reused as cook liquor; 7 cycles demonstrated; COD rises then stabilizes from the 5th reuse at ~60,000-60,713 mg/L; pH stable 8.5-9.2. Declared outcome: no liquid effluent leaves the process. 2
  9. Legal / industrial status — patent PI 0305004-1 A filed 2003-10-06, granted as BR 0305004 B1 on 2014-11-11, holder UnB; licensed to Poiato Recicla, pilot since March 2016, "some tons" processed, pulp sold to a cardboard factory. 1

parameter not located overall: pulp yield per kg butts; pulp washing parameters; bleaching; eventual disposal of saturated reuse liquor; sodium balance of the loop.

2. Equipment

  • Pilot (as used): 50 L stainless steel reactor, LPG flame heating, digital thermocouple 2. Lab replication: 2-5 L glass reactor or stainless pot with reflux condenser and electric hotplate (electric heating replaces LPG — this plan's substitution, safety note 4).
  • Jokro mill (25 min refine) and TAPPI sheet former for paper QC 2 — any standard pulp lab has equivalents; substitutions are a gap to declare (row 10).
  • Vacuum filtration setup, pH meter, COD photometer (closed reflux, SMWW 5220D as used) for liquor tracking.
  • FTIR (or partner lab access) to verify deacetylation (1760 cm-1 band) — the source's own monitoring tool.
  • No pressure equipment: every source run is atmospheric; the patent's 100-atm envelope must NOT be replicated (safety note 6).

4. Safety notes

  • Caustic liquor: NaOH 1% cook and pH-11 bleach are corrosive; face shield, nitrile gloves, add alkali to water. Boiling NaOH liquor foams — headspace >=50% of vessel volume. 2
  • Dark liquor is the hazard center of this route: nicotine 35.4 g/L (dermally toxic), NNK 3.1 mg/L (carcinogen), COD ~30 g/L. All transfers in closed or hooded containers; nitrile gloves; no skin contact; every drop to the reuse loop or licensed disposal, never to drain. 2
  • Acidification: HCl 6 N — fumes and exotherm; add acid slowly, ventilated area. 2
  • Heating: source used LPG flame; replace with electric hotplate (flammable-gas substitution is this plan's choice, not the source's).
  • Ozone step: do not replicate — the source itself shows it ineffective (COD often rose); the hazard (oxidant gas) buys nothing.
  • Pressure: patent claims run to 100 atm 1; every demonstrated run is atmospheric. No pressure vessels — the charter's standing ban applies.
  • Lignin precipitate and coagulation sludge: concentrate nicotine/metals; hazardous waste stream, licensed disposal.
  • Material stop-condition: metals are characterized in liquor (favorable) but NOT in pulp (row 8) — route on hold for product-facing work until ICP-MS passes.
  • Desk research authorizes no physical trial: qualified facility, risk assessment, lawful waste handling, named human actor.

5. What the team must verify in lab

Ordered by information value:

  1. Lab cook + mass balance (100 g butts, 10 L/kg, 1% NaOH, 3 h boil): FT-IR deacetylation check, washed-dry pulp mass. Confirms/kills rows 2, 3, 4. Acceptance: acetate bands gone; pulp 25-45% of feed.
  2. Hand sheet vs source Table 4: grammage, thickness, whiteness, tensile. Acceptance: same order as source values (traction index 5-12 kNm/kg). Confirms the paper-quality premise end-to-end (rows 10, and row 5 if pH<9 rinse used).
  3. Liquor triangle: COD + nicotine + 6-metal ICP on raw liquor; acidify pH 3 -> COD removal ~20% (source value); reuse x3 with COD tracking. Confirms/kills rows 7, 9. Decision rule: COD trend parallel to 2 Table 11 -> loop works; sodium/conductivity runaway -> size a purge.
  4. Metals gate: ICP-MS on washed pulp (row 8). Gate for any product-facing claim.
  5. Bleach trial (row 6) only if a lighter grade than cardboard is the goal.
