0. Summary card

The route in one paragraph. INV-01 is a pilot route built entirely from verified components already on the shelf: the M-01 float/sink water split and three-tank wash, the M-02 polarity-graded polish, the M-03 alkaline cook with its demonstrated closed dark-liquor loop, and the M-07 pyrolysis destructor with the Chemosphere 2026 two-stage frame. Every input kilogram is directed into five weighed, assayable streams — S1 CA product, S2 pulp, S3 liquor loop, S4 toxin concentrate, S5 pyrolysis outputs — so the route's core deliverable is the complete toxicant mass balance.

Why that is the deliverable. The field's biggest documented failure is the missing mass balance: Chemosphere 2026 found NO complete toxicant mass balance in 37 screened studies, and INERIS (2018) faulted even the best-in-class route (UnB) for exactly that — "ne réalise pas de bilan de masse des polluants et ne présente aucun résultat d'analyse chimique portant sur le flux de pâte cellulosique". INV-01 turns that failure into the method's core output.

How the metals question is handled — routed, not hidden. The wet stages do not remove bound metals, so the concentrate and char streams are where metals are PREDICTED to accumulate, and the ICP-MS gate decides before anything leaves the lab.

What this document is not. An invention is not a claim of novelty, patentability, safety or deployability; every assumption below is ai proposal/ai inference with a falsifier, and desk research authorizes no physical trial.

1. Mechanism and why it is different from existing registry entries

Mechanism, per toxin class (ai inference from shelf sources; show-your-chain):

  • Nicotine and water-soluble alkaloids — removed by DISSOLUTION, not destruction: nicotine is freely water-soluble, so the 40-50 degC first wash (M-01 row 1) and the NaOH cook liquor (M-03: 35.4 g/L measured in dark liquor) carry it into S3; the chitosan coagulation step then removes it to ND (Teixeira 2017, coagulation table) and the bleed keeps the loop gradient alive (M-01 row 7 proposal).
  • Tar/PAH/semi-polars — polarity-graded extraction: water stages miss them (M-01 admits "another type of solvent"), ethanol 99% stages take them (M-02 wash sequence); whatever survives goes to S4 and is THERMALLY DESTROYED in S5 (nicotine decomposes from 247 degC per INERIS 2018; afterburn envelope 850 degC / 2 s adopted from the same source).
  • Metals (Cd/Pb/As/Hg) — NOT REMOVED by any wet stage (no chelation in the base design). They are ROUTED: the design predicts they concentrate in S4 (clarifloculation + lignin + chitosan sludges) and then in S5 char. This prediction is the route's metals falsifier, and the charter stop-condition still binds all product-facing work until ICP-MS passes.
  • Cellulose acetate — preserved as polymer in S1 (float; water is a non-solvent for CA), intentionally converted to cellulose pulp ONLY where the sink stream enters the cook (M-03 deacetylation: acetate IR bands gone in 10 min at 80 degC / 1% NaOH).

Why it is different from every registry entry:

Registry entry What it leaves unanswered What INV-01 does instead
M-01 water wash No answer for tar (its own admission), for its recycler sludge, or for metals Adds the ethanol polish, the bleed-with-cap rule, and makes the sludge a NAMED stream (S4)
M-03 UnB pulping Cooks the WHOLE butt and thereby destroys the CA polymer (60.1% mass kept as cellulose — computed) to make low-grade pulp, while running the strongest liquor loop Keeps CA polymer intact by splitting the feed before the cook
M-02 De Fenzo wash Proves product quality but is grams-scale with batch solvents and no waste story Keeps the polish as a stage, not a route, and pays the solvent cost only on the S1 stream
M-07 pyrolysis Converts everything thermally with no mass balance of toxins and a 200 g ceiling Uses pyrolysis only where it is strong — destroying a small, concentrated, pre-characterized tail — and imports the two-stage frame
TerraCycle-style routes Keep the decontamination step a trade secret; Bloomberg 2022 documents the storage/disposal problem that a closed loop avoids Closes the loop: every extracted pollutant has a named destination (S4 -> S5)

Three things no existing entry does. No existing entry (1) splits the feed BEFORE cooking, (2) reuses one liquor train across BOTH the wash and the cook, or (3) gives the concentrated hazardous tail a designed destructor stage.

Relation to the prior project hypotheses. The prior project hypotheses (candidate-hypotheses-01 H1/H2) proposed staged washing and contaminant-rejecting recovery symbolically; INV-01 is the concrete instantiation with numbers and a falsifier ledger.

2. Full process spec (10 kg dried whole-butt pilot cycle; every number labeled)

Feed basis. Composition 62/20/18 wt% filter/paper/tobacco is source supported for Keenat-style feed (M-07 measured) and MUST be re-measured on the team's local feed before any yield band is trusted (ai inference: feedstock heterogeneity shifts decomposition windows ~40-50 degC and leachate COD up to 314% per Chemosphere 2026 — publish the feed boundary with every run).

  • Stage A — dry prep (M-01 steps 3-4): manual triage, mill ~1 kg/min-class (bench equivalent fine at 10 kg/day), three-level sieve. ai proposal: collect the fines (<500 um) sub-stream separately — ash-heavy, predicted metals-rich, direct to S4. Optional variant (prior project proposal, inherited): dry air-classification of fines before wetting, falsified if >25% of any metal stays on the CA tow (ICP-MS).
  • Stage B — float/sink split (M-01 step 5): water, density separation, >=20-30 min, first tank at 40-50 degC (ai proposal, M-01 row 1), paper sinks, CA floats. Water 8.3 L/kg first pass = 83 L for 10 kg (ai inference, computed from M-01's disclosed 1000 L / 120 kg cycle). Paper-wet sink drained (not dried) straight into Stage D.
  • Stage C — CA polish (M-01 step 6 + M-02 stages 4-7): two more wash tanks 30 min ambient, then ethanol 99% x2 ambient (ai proposal: M-02 ratio 1:10 w/v), dry 60 degC to <=5% moisture (ai proposal, M-01 row 8). Product S1: CA fine particles (<1500 um target, M-01 step 9). Optional acetone film/plate trial only after the metals gate (M-02 casting, 20 wt% triacetin plasticizer proposal).
  • Stage D — alkaline cook of the SINK (M-03 rows 1-3): the drained sink (paper + tobacco + CA fines + ash) cooked in NaOH 1% w/v, 10 L/kg, 80 degC, 3 h, electric heating, atmospheric (never the patent's 100-atm envelope). Output: washed pulp (S2, vacuum-filter + 3x10 L/kg water rinses to pH<9, M-03 row 5 proposal) + dark liquor into Stage E. THE UNTESTED VARIANT: Teixeira cooked whole butts, where CA dominated the pulp; the sink stream is paper/tobacco-dominated, so pulp yield/quality is the route's top falsifier (see 9.3).
  • Stage E — unified liquor loop (S3): ONE train for wash water AND dark liquor, the invention's central integration. Sequence per cycle: (1) acidify pH 3 with HCl — lignin precipitation, 20.1% COD removal (source supported, Teixeira); (2) chitosan 0.80 g/L at pH 9, jar-test regime — 66.6% COD removal, nicotine ND (source supported, Teixeira); (3) neutralize pH 7 and reuse as cook liquor + first-stage wash water — 7 reuse cycles demonstrated for the cook-liquor role (source supported, Teixeira Table 11), the wash role is new (ai proposal); (4) bleed 20%/cycle at the 50 mg/L recycled-nicotine cap (M-01 row 7 proposal), make-up ~17 L/cycle (computed). Sodium acetate accumulates: 0.47-0.94 kg NaOAc per 10-kg cycle depending on CA-in-sink share, consuming 0.23-0.46 kg NaOH stoichiometrically (computed from M-03 row 7 factors) — tracked, not assumed away (M-03 row 7 falsifier).
  • Stage F — pyrolytic end-of-life (S5): S4 = ALL concentrated hazardous solids — clarifloculation sludge (M-01 step 7), lignin precipitate + chitosan sludge (Stage E), Stage A fines, spent ethanol/extract residues, dewatered. ai proposal 3-7 wt% of feed = 0.3-0.7 kg dry per cycle. Processed in a QUALIFIED furnace (no DIY thermal equipment), two stages per the Chemosphere 2026 frame and M-07 numbers: (1) desorption ramp 150-200 degC, N2, ~30 min hold — condensate captured BEFORE nicotine decomposition (247 degC, INERIS) — predicted to carry most recoverable triacetin (desorption band 100-300 degC, source supported Chemosphere; capture-yield UNTESTED — falsifier 9.5); (2) conversion at 650 degC (char-maximizing choice, M-07 row 5; 25 degC/min-class ramp, N2, 5-10 min hold) producing Ca-rich char; off-gas afterburned 850 degC / 2 s (INERIS incineration envelope). Char and condensate are assay streams, not products: char faces the ICP-MS gate before any adsorbent or additive claim; condensate goes to calibrated GC-MS assay then licensed disposal unless the team's lab proves otherwise.

4. What existing methods fail at, and how this addresses it

  • M-01 alone: no tar answer (its own text), closed-loop drift unmanaged, recycler sludge undefined, metals untouched. INV-01 adds the ethanol polish, the bleed-with-cap rule, and makes the sludge a NAMED stream (S4).
  • M-03 alone: cooks the CA polymer into low-grade pulp (60.1% mass retention, computed) and its saturated-liquor disposal is parameter not located in the sources. INV-01 keeps CA polymer intact by splitting first, and routes the liquor's own concentrates to a destructor instead of an undefined disposal.
  • M-02 alone: grams-scale, batch solvents, no waste accounting. INV-01 keeps its polish as a stage, not a route, and pays the solvent cost only on the S1 stream.
  • M-07 alone: whole-butt pyrolysis has no toxicant mass balance, 200 g ceiling, oil not blend-ready. INV-01 uses pyrolysis only where it is strong — destroying a small, concentrated, pre-characterized tail — and imports the two-stage frame so triacetin recovery is attempted BEFORE destruction.
  • Field-level (Chemosphere 2026 verdicts): no complete toxicant balance anywhere, no pilot, no leaching tests, no butts-only LCA. INV-01 is pilot-shaped (10 kg), mass-balance-first, and generates the exact assay dataset a future LCA would need. It does not claim to BE an LCA, a TEA, or a validated process — it produces the inputs they require.
  • Keenat's operational death (see benchmark 2) is answered structurally: the two failure causes Keenat named each get a designed response, not an assurance.

5. Mandatory benchmark answers

  • Korean paving-stone test (toxin removal mechanism): answered by the per-class mechanism table in section 1 — dissolution+coagulation for nicotine/alkaloids, polarity-graded solvation for tar/PAHs, thermal destruction for the surviving tail, ROUTING (not removal) for metals. Provenance honesty: the benchmark's name is inherited from inventions/TEMPLATE.md; the live registry's Korean entries are C-12 (KR20160005298A, extraction of useful compounds) and C-15 (KR microbial composting) — no Korean paving-stone card exists in the current tree, so the name is treated as retired-workflow legacy and answered by its stated function (name the removal mechanism).
  • Keenat LCA benchmark (energy/inputs/waste accounting): Keenat's own LCA killed their route on "prohibitive operating costs" and "persistent uncertainty on long-term management of extracted pollutants" (M-04, company self-report). INV-01's answers, labeled as proposals not claims: (a) accounting — the route's dominant inputs are water (83 L first cycle, ~17 L/cycle steady), NaOH 1% cook, ethanol polish on S1 only, and the S5 furnace; the highest-cost unknown is the S5 destructor, which is sized to the SMALL concentrate (0.3-0.7 kg per 10 kg feed, 3-7 wt%) rather than the whole stream — the structural cost answer Keenat never had; (b) long-term fate — every extracted pollutant has a named destination (S4 -> S5 -> char-for-assay or licensed disposal; condensate -> assay -> licensed disposal), replacing "persistent uncertainty" with assayable streams. Energy per kg is parameter not located until the team logs kWh (M-07 row 7 falsifier method); no cost or cleanliness claim is made.
  • INERIS waste-handling benchmark: INERIS 2018's critique of the field's best route is the invention's founding document — no pollutant mass balance, no chemical analysis of the pulp. INV-01's core deliverable IS that balance (section 3) plus per-stream assays, with the pulp explicitly in the assay list. INERIS's own envelope for thermal disposal (850 degC / 2 s; nicotine decomposes from 247 degC) is adopted verbatim for S5's off-gas; all liquors stay in the loop or go to licensed disposal, never to drain (INERIS hazardous-waste framing; charter safety rule).

7. Home-validation experiments (what the human can test, pass/fail criteria)

  1. Float/sink efficiency (100 g batch): weigh sunken paper, count paper residual in floated CA at 30x. PASS: <10% residual paper after pass 1 (M-01 row 3 criterion). Kills Stage B sizing if failed.
  2. Mass-balance diary: every stream weighed every run, S3 inventory logged. PASS: closure statement reconciles within weighing error. This is the invention's habit-forming core.
  3. Sink-pulp sheet trial (Stage D on 200 g sink): blender-refine, hand mold. PASS: sheet holds together, tensile same order as Teixeira Table 4 band (traction index 5-12 kNm/kg at partner lab). FALSIFIES 9.3 if it collapses.
  4. Moisture gravimetric (105 degC to constant mass). PASS: <=5%.
  5. Loop drift, bench version: 5 reuse cycles of the wash liquor with COD strips + nicotine assays at a partner lab. PASS: plateau below the 50 mg/L cap (M-01 row 7 rule). NOT home-testable by design: any toxin clearance (no odor/appearance judgment — charter rule), pyrolysis, metals — all partner-lab with named human actor.

8. Safety notes

  • Dark liquor / spent washes carry nicotine up to 35.4 g/L measured (M-03) — dermally acutely toxic; closed transfer, nitrile gloves, eye protection; NEVER to drain; all to S3 loop or licensed disposal.
  • NaOH 1% cook + pH-11 wash chemistry corrosive; HCl 6 N acidification fumes/exotherm — add acid slowly, ventilated.
  • Ethanol 99% (flash ~13 degC) and acetone (flash ~-20 degC, film variant): fume hood, grounded, no ignition sources.
  • Dry organic dust (<1500 um CA particles, Stage A fines) is an explosible dust class: enclosed mill, LEV, no fines accumulation.
  • Stage F runs at 650 degC on toxic waste: CO measured at 8% of product gas in M-07, HCN class unmeasured; qualified facility, afterburn 850/2s, CO + HCN monitoring mandatory; condenser tars are S4.
  • No pressure vessels anywhere (the 100-atm claim envelope is NOT replicated); no DIY thermal equipment.
  • Hazardous waste streams, all named: S4 solids, S5 char (pre-gate), S5 condensate, lignin/chitosan/clarifloculation sludges, spent ethanol extracts. Characterize before disposal route selection.
  • Metals stop-condition binds the whole route: Cd/Pb/As/Hg uncharacterised in S1/S2 products = on hold for product-facing work, regardless of wash/polish results (ICP-MS gate, M-01 row 11 / M-02 row 9 / M-03 row 8 precedent).
  • Desk research authorizes no physical trial: qualified facility, risk assessment, lawful waste handling, named human actor before any step runs.

9. Falsifier ledger (load-bearing claims, ranked)

  1. Sink-pulp quality — Teixeira's pulp was CA-derived; the sink stream is paper/tobacco-dominated. FALSIFIED if Stage D pulp fails the Table-4-band sheet test after ratio adjustment (10 -> 32 L/kg). Fallback: drop Stage D, route sink to S4/S5 — the route survives as M-01+M-02 with a destructor tail.
  2. Unified-loop compatibility — cook liquor and wash water have different COD/nicotine loads; sharing one train is new. FALSIFIED if the 5-cycle drift test shows monotonic rise past the 50 mg/L cap at 20% bleed, or sodium/conductivity runaway (M-03 row 7) — then split trains or raise the bleed.
  3. Split purity — FALSIFIED if float CA carries >10% paper after one pass or visible fines after two (M-01 row 3 test).
  4. Metals partition prediction — the design CLAIMS metals concentrate in S4/S5. FALSIFIED if ICP-MS shows metals above gate in S1 or S2 — which also trips the charter stop-condition; the route then produces characterization data, not products.
  5. Two-stage condensate value — FALSIFIED if calibrated GC-MS finds triacetin mostly NOT in the 150-200 degC condensate; recovery is then abandoned and Stage F remains destruction-only (the route does not depend on the recovery).
  6. S4 mass band — FALSIFIED if concentrates exceed 10 wt% of feed (3-7% proposed): the destructor sizing and the whole cost argument weaken; re-size before any scale claim.
