Exact title

Pyrolysis of cigarette butts as a sustainable strategy to recover triacetin for low-cost and efficient biodiesel production

Publication / identifier

Journal of Analytical and Applied Pyrolysis 175 (2023) 106167. DOI 10.1016/j.jaap.2023.106167. Authors: Samy Yousef (Kaunas University of Technology), Justas Eimontas, Kęstutis Zakarauskas, Nerijus Striūgas (Lithuanian Energy Institute). Funded by Research Council of Lithuania, agreement S-MIP-23-118. Data availability: confidential.

Relevance note

The M-07 primary source, now at full strength: 200 g fixed-bed reactor, 650/700/750 degC, 25 degC/min, N2 60 ml/min, 5-10 min hold, 3 repetitions; feedstock 62 wt% filter / 20 wt% paper / 18 wt% tobacco supplied by KEENAT Company France (cross-link to M-04); Table 1 exact yields (oil 37.92-39.52 / char 25.68-27.45 / gas 33.03-36.40 wt%, sums = 100); triacetin 31.23 / 33.26 / 42.99 GC-MS area% at 650/700/750; alkaloid (nicotine-derived pyridine) peaks present in the oil (safety-relevant); char EDX: Ca 2.15 -> 31.85 wt% with temperature, plus K, Fe, S, Cl, Si, Mg, P; CaCO3 FTIR bands; pores <1 um; oil blend guidance up to 25% (triacetin allowance 10%); LCA + techno-economics declared future work.
