Relevance note

The original trade-press process description that later summaries (including Wikipedia) cite: filters recycled into pellets for park benches, shipping pallets and railroad ties (ashtrays also named); paper and tobacco composted; development took "nearly two years"; 100 TerraCycle points per pound of waste (redeemable at 1 cent per point for charity); Keep America Beautiful 2009: cigarette refuse = 38% of U.S. roadway litter. Cellulose acetate named as the filter polymer.
