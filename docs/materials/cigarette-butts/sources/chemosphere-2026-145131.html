Exact title

Thermal conversion of waste cigarette butts: Feedstock heterogeneity, process control, valorization pathways, and environmental safety: A critical review

Publication / identifier

Chemosphere (2026), article 145131. DOI 10.1016/j.chemosphere.2026.145131. Authors: Han Zhang, Jianyuan Fu, Bolin Wang, Hanjun Hu, Libo Zhang (Guangdong University of Petrochemical Technology; University of Science and Technology Beijing; North China Electric Power University; Beijing Chaobai Environmental Technology). Funded by NSFC 22368051 and Guangdong provincial programs. 66 references; 37 studies screened for environmental-safety endpoints. DeepSeek-R1 acknowledged for language editing.

Relevance note

The post-dating cross-check for M-07: CONFIRMS the Yousef 2023 numbers we hold (whole-butt pyrolysis 650-750 degC: oil 38-39.5 wt%, char 25.7-27.7, gas 33-36.4; triacetin 43% of oil at 750 degC — explicitly characterized as enrichment within the mixed oil, NOT isolated purity) and adds the gas composition at 750 degC (CO2 11.6 / CO 8.0 / H2 4.6 / CH4 2.0 vol%). Reconciles staged behavior: triacetin thermally desorbs at 100-300 degC and cellulose acetate pyrolyzes at 301-400 degC (H. Kim 2024, acetic acid ~40% with Al-MCM-41 in the desorption stage only) — 650-750 degC is reactor set-temperature, not triacetin release temperature. Field-level verdicts that anchor M-07's open rows: NO complete mass balance of nicotine/PAHs/metals across gas/oil/char/ash/wastewater in ANY of 37 screened studies; no standardized leaching, ecotoxicity, or oil-toxicity testing reported; no pilot-scale demonstration (200 g fixed bed is the largest whole-butt reactor); no complete TEA; no independent LCA with butts as sole feedstock; conventional pyrolysis judged the near-term baseline route with highest scale-up potential. Catalog of adjacent routes with numbers: Lee 2025 (filters, 500 degC fixed bed: 53.3% oil), Duanguppama 2023 (smoked butts 400 degC: 33.3% liquid; +dolomite: 28.6% liquid, HHV 12.8->18.1 MJ/kg), Yousef 2025 (oxygen-fed updraft gasification + 900 degC catalytic reforming: syngas yield 82.3-89.7%, H2 30.27 vol%, tar 18.15->13.38 g/m3), Premchand 2025 (slow pyrolysis char 26.6->20.1 wt% over 400-700 degC, BET 272.9-270.8 m2/g, Ni(II)/Cu(II) adsorption), Herrera-Puerta 2025 (350 degC + KOH: BET 433 m2/g, Cr(VI) 55.8 mg/g), Guo 2025 (Ni/char pyrolysis-reforming: 75% conversion at 700 degC).
