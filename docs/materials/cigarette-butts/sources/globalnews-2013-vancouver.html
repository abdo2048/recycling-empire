Relevance note

FIRST on-record TerraCycle staff disclosure of the gamma step (2013, four years before the Surfrider FAQ): Albe Zakes (Global VP): "The left over butts are gamma radiated, which is the same process that is used on fish once it's pulled out of the sea, to kill any bio contaminants. Once they have been gamma radiated, they can be turned into plastic pellets... industrial-only products such as plastic lumber, shipping pallets... no consumer products will be made from the material." Also: world's first city butt-recycling program (Vancouver, 110 lamp-post receptacles, emptied by United We Can, $5/lb donated to United We Can vs US $1/lb; material shipped to TerraCycle Toronto).
