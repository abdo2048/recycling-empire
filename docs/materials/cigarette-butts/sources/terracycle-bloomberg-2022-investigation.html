Relevance note

The independent-check source for M-06's credibility rows: TerraCycle revenue $71M (2021, company-stated); TerraCycle does NO recycling itself — all reprocessing contracted to third parties; no independent recycling audit until 2021 (Bureau Veritas engaged thereafter); downcycling of contaminated flakes into playground surfaces / outdoor furniture; tracker evidence of material going to landfill (Pontiac, Ill.) and 20+ bales misrouted to Bulgaria; Bloomington lawsuit vs Bell International LLC / Akshar Plastic; Jan Dell false-advertising suit settled with language changes. Confirms the cigarette-butt magic-show description (paper -> paper recycling, tobacco -> compost, plastic -> pellets/flakes). Still NO cigarette-specific tonnages or process parameters.
